## Featured work

### FruitStock — wholesale business management

I built and deployed FruitStock for a wholesale client to bring inventory, purchases, sales, customer/dealer balances and payments into one application.

- React and TypeScript frontend connected to an Express API.
- PostgreSQL and Prisma for business records and transaction-based updates.
- JWT authentication and dashboards for sales, stock value, receivables and payables.
- Frontend deployment on Vercel, with the backend and database on Railway.

The engineering challenge I focused on was keeping stock movements and account balances consistent when business transactions change.

[Source code](https://github.com/tanishqasd/fruitstock-pro) · [Application sign-in](https://fruitwholeasaler.vercel.app/)

The live application requires a business owner account. I do not share client credentials or business records publicly.
